Send a request

For a bug report, include the app version and build, iPhone or Watch model, OS version and steps to reproduce it. For a privacy request, briefly identify information you already shared with the provider. Keep passwords, health values and complete training archives private. A redacted screenshot is optional.

Privacy requests and rights

Use the privacy contact to ask the provider about access to information you already shared, correction or deletion. Where applicable, you can also request restriction or portability, object to processing or withdraw consent. You can complain to a competent data protection authority.
