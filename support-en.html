Common questions

How do I change workout days or my plan?

Open Profile → Build a new plan to go through guided planning. The new plan becomes active when you apply it. On days off, Today shows a rest day and your next workout.

What if the Watch cannot confirm a change?

Open KAIROS Evolve on the paired iPhone and Watch. Wait for the Watch to confirm its current state. When disconnected, you can view the last confirmed state. A change is shown as saved after the iPhone confirms it.

Why are Apple Health values missing?

Check KAIROS Evolve permissions in Apple Health and whether values are visible for the requested period. You can enter body measurements manually. Water transfer has separate permission. An empty result does not confirm a measured zero.

How do I move training records to a new device?

Create a JSON export under Profile → Your data. On the new device, select file import, review the preview and confirm it. From version 0.7.0, the app excludes its training archives from automatic device backups. The export can contain personal information.

How do I remove a bottle or a training archive?

Touch and hold the bottle button and select the event. When connected to Apple Health, the app attempts to delete its own matching sample. Under Profile → Your data, you can erase the selected archive and its local backups. Manage exported files and existing Apple Health records separately.

What does the calorie display mean?

It shows an experimental estimate of active energy for the whole session. The details show the time components and the basis for the estimate. Use the numbers and workout suggestions as guidance for training.
