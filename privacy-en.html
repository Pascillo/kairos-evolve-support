Purpose and basic approach

KAIROS Evolve helps you plan, log and review strength training. It does not require an account. Its regular features process your training and profile data in the app's storage on your device. There is no dedicated server for these data, built-in advertising or analytics network, or cross-app tracking. The app does not sell your data or use health data for advertising.

Information stored locally

You choose which optional information to enter. Depending on your use, your archive can include:

  • Profile and setup draft: optional name, adult confirmation, age, height, weight, experience, goals, training and rest days, time budget, selected muscles, language, units and exercise preferences. Voluntary information about limitations, other sports and training breaks can also be saved.
  • Training plans, custom exercises, and locations and equipment you name yourself. A training location is a label you enter; the app does not obtain GPS coordinates for it.
  • Recorded training: exercises and order, set type, load and its convention, repetitions, timestamps, measured set and rest durations, start and finish, notes, and optional information such as repetitions in reserve, technique or pain. Plan versions, original targets and explicitly logged corrections can remain in the archive.
  • Optional recovery check-ins linked to previous actual effort: an overall self-rating, soreness by muscle or body region, separately recorded unusual pain, and choices such as skipping or reminding later. Resulting advice and any explicitly chosen adjustment to the current workout are local as well.
  • Intentionally skipped planned workouts with their scheduled date, workout, selected reason and, where applicable, a reference to an existing recovery check-in. Undo retains a local correction marker for future backup imports. These entries can be included in JSON exports and are processed locally.
  • Water events with amount and time, an optional workout reference, and the status of requested Apple Health writes or deletions.
  • Optional body information, an optional sex setting and calculation settings for the experimental calorie estimate, and saved estimates with their original inputs. A later profile change does not automatically replace those historical inputs.

The app also keeps preferences in its local settings storage. Demo and personal training archives remain separate. The body map does not require a camera image, body photograph or medical examination. Its markings are your own reports, not a diagnosis.

The optional watch app exchanges workout information directly with your paired iPhone. This can include the daily plan, exercise and equipment names, set targets, repetitions, loads, timing information, a stated reason for skipping a workout and a brief completion summary. Logging or correcting a set can also exchange optional repetitions in reserve, technique, discomfort and set notes. The watch keeps the last confirmed state and its timestamp locally. The full training archive and values read from Apple Health remain on the iPhone. Without a confirmed connection, the watch can show its last received state. It shows changes as saved after the iPhone confirms them.

Apple Health is optional

The app requests Apple Health data only with your permission. You can use manual features without Health access. Apple controls which data are visible; an empty result does not establish that no data exist.

During setup, the app can read height, the latest visible body weight, and age calculated from date of birth. You review and select values before importing them. Imported values can be saved in your setup draft and then your profile. The complete date of birth is not stored in the training archive. A weight measurement date may be retained to provide context for that value.

A separate Health view can show a summary of existing traditional and functional strength workouts from the last 90 days and, optionally, read the latest visible weight. These query views are temporary; saving a weight in your profile requires your explicit choice. Reading Health workouts does not automatically import them as logged KAIROS Evolve sessions.

Before a scheduled workout, you can view carbohydrate entries visible in Apple Health over the last four hours. This separate read request processes the amount, timestamp and source app temporarily on your iPhone. If several sources are visible, you choose one. The values are excluded from the training archive and its exports and are never sent to the Apple Watch or other apps.

Write access is requested separately for dietary water. If you enable automatic water transfer and Apple grants permission, new bottle events can be written to Health with their amount, time and a technical identifier. Existing pending entries are transferred only when you choose the separate action for them. Other apps can access those data only with their own Health read permissions. The app does not write workout calories, sets, body measurements, recovery check-ins or pain markings to Health.

You can turn off water transfer in the app and change Health permissions in system settings or Health. Revocation prevents future access; it does not remove existing Health entries or local profile copies. Previously requested deletions of the app's own water entries can still be reconciled when permission is available.

Retention, export and deletion

Archives remain until you explicitly erase them. Saving can create a local backup of the previous archive. An unreadable file can be preserved as a local recovery copy. Local recovery preserves known deletion markers for water entries and undone workout skips. An older backup may not include workouts or changes added in the meantime.

Under Profile → Your data, you can export a JSON archive, review an import or erase the selected mode's data. An export can include all body, training, recovery, pain and note information in that archive, including historical calculation inputs, correction and deletion markers, and technical status fields. It is not an anonymized file. You choose where to save or share it through system dialogs. The developer does not receive it automatically.

Erasing a mode removes its local archive and local backup and recovery copies. The other mode is preserved. General app preferences can remain. The watch receives the updated state when it synchronizes again. Until then, a disconnected watch can show the last confirmed state. Previously exported files, device backups and data already written to Health are not automatically erased by this action.

Touch and hold the bottle button to remove individual water events. They disappear from the displayed total; local deletion markers remain for reconciliation. The app also attempts to delete the corresponding Health samples it created. If permission is missing or an error occurs, Health deletion remains pending and is retried later. Other apps' samples are not deleted. You can also manage existing data directly in Apple Health.

From version 0.7.0, the app excludes its training archives, local archive copies and the Watch workout cache from automatic device backups. To move your records to another device, create an export in the app and keep it in a secure location you choose. Earlier device backups and your export destinations remain under your control and are subject to the provider’s terms.

On-device explanations and voluntary feedback

On supported devices, the optional coach explanation can use Apple's on-device Foundation Models model. Your question and the supplied rule texts are processed on your device. KAIROS Evolve does not send them to its own or another provider's cloud AI service. Rule-based explanations remain available without the model.

If you voluntarily contact the provider or share files or screenshots for troubleshooting, these can contain personal information. Share only what is necessary; a training archive is not required for an ordinary bug report. Voluntarily submitted support material is used only to address your request and is deleted after the request is completed. It is not used for advertising or sale. Feedback in the TestFlight system is also subject to Apple's processing described below.

TestFlight-specific processing

With TestFlight, Apple automatically collects beta usage information and crash reports and shares them and your feedback with the provider. Depending on the invitation method, this can include your name and email address. Apple retains beta feedback for one year and may retain crash and usage information until bugs are resolved.

The provider works within the area supplied by Apple. KAIROS Evolve has no separate feedback database. The provider uses beta information only to fix problems and improve the app and does not disclose it to third parties. Avoid unnecessary health information in feedback screenshots.

Source: Apple · TestFlight & Privacy.

Your controls and updates

You can omit optional information, change profile settings, revoke Health permissions, skip or disable recovery check-ins, and export or erase local archives. Editing profile values does not automatically remove past workout snapshots or logged corrections. For requests about information actually sent to the provider, use the privacy contact described above. Your applicable privacy rights remain available to you. The developer has no remote access to archives that exist only on your device.

This statement describes the identified feature version. Changes to processing will be published with an updated date and a clear explanation. It does not provide a medical assessment or guarantee training safety or effectiveness.
